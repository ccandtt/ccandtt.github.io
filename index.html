Hi, I'm Xinchang.

I'm Xinchang Wang, a PhD student jointly trained by Fudan University and Beijing Zhongguancun Academy (2026 cohort). I am advised by Prof. Xingjun Ma at Fudan University and Prof. Haoxuan Li at Beijing Zhongguancun Academy.

I am currently exploring causal inference and AI agents.

I completed my undergraduate studies in Artificial Intelligence at Jiangnan University, where I conducted research under the supervision of Prof. Hui Li. My previous work includes RA-Det and MambaGuard for AI-generated image detection.
